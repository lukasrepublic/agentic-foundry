## FG-OPS-004 - Production changes are announced before and reported after

Before each production or irreversible write, the actor MUST post one line that states what will happen, with a correlation id. After it, the actor MUST post the outcome with the same correlation id. A missed before line MUST be reported as a process miss. On a failure the actor MUST stop, clean up what it created, and report. Subagents MUST inherit this rule.
